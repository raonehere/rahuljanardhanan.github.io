# Mere Junior / Lyrical Animation

Amit Bhadana's "Mere Junior" is an incredibly sentimental tune. Prior to its release, Amit reached out to me for a rapid creation of a lyrical animation video. Despite the tight time constraints, I managed to produce the illustrations and animations for the project. The end result exceeded expectations and left both parties thoroughly satisfied.
