# Widget concepts for NothingOS

NothingOS is an Android skin developed for smartphones by the London-based company ‘Nothing’. It aims to minimize distractions and encourage intentional smartphone use. Widgets are a key feature through which Nothing seeks to reduce app usage. The approach to widgets and their design by Nothing particularly intrigued me, prompting me to explore various concepts. I shared my exploration as a series on Twitter, which garnered significant attention and provided valuable insights and perspectives.
