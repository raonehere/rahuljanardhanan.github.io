# OP Konzept / Logo Animation

OP Konzept is an organic paint brand from Kerala, working to make paint safer for people and the planet. They use natural pigments to bring color in a way that’s less toxic and more in tune with nature. I had the chance to team up with **Pixpil Agency** to bring their logo to life through animation as part of their visual identity.
