# Willmount, Invitation card

Willmount is a travel and experience agency specializing in Kerala. They approached me to design an invitation letter for their innovative geodesic dome stay. Following their request, I created a flat yet vibrant illustration-based invitation card that encapsulated the essence of their new property.
