# ONDC Network / Animated Explainer

The Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC) serves as a platform connecting online buyers and sellers. Established by the Government of India's DPIIT, it's a private, non-profit entity. Through Media Monk Agency, I was tasked with fully animating an explainer video showcasing how the ONDC network operates, slated for presentation at a conference in Singapore.
