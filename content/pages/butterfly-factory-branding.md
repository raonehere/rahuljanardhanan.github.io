# Butterfly Factory, Branding

The Butterfly Factory is a small business creating upcycled handmade sketchbooks, journals, gifts, artworks etc. By transforming what's considered waste or unusable, into functional creations, they give a second life to materials that otherwise end up in landfills. I helped them to create a brand identity that conveys what they are and at the same time bring out their personality.
![](../media/butterfly-factory-branding/01.jpg)
I've created three logo variations to suit different settings and scenarios where it will be used.
