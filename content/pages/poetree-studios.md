# Poetree Studios

Poetree Studios is a space where artists, students and art lovers come together and can freely explore and express their creativity. By creating a brand logo for Poetree, I've helped them comunicate their mission, which is to create, learn and share art.
![](../media/poetree-studios/01.jpg)
![](../media/poetree-studios/02.jpg)
Poetree Studios came to me with the concept of crafting a logo centered around typography, complemented by organic elements like a spiral. Despite its complexity, I was instantly captivated by the concept and produced this design.
