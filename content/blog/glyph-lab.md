Glyph Lab is a growing collection of home screen widgets and interactive Glyph Matrix toys for Nothing phones, made by Vimanam Studio. It started with a simple question: the Glyph LEDs on the back of a Nothing phone can do more than blink for notifications. What if they became a small screen for play?

The app is built around two ideas. The first is useful things you look at every day, placed right on the home screen. The second is tiny, playful things that use the Glyph Matrix and Always On Display.

## Widgets

- **Music Player** — a now-playing widget with Cassette, Classic vinyl, and 90s Widamp themes, with playback controls and animated visualizers.
- **System Info** — live CPU, RAM, storage, network, and battery readouts, with an animated charging stream.
- **Walkie Talkie** — push-to-talk voice memos with friends, right from the home screen, with unread memos queued up.
- **Caffeinate** — keep the screen awake for a set time, as a round 1×1 or pill-shaped 2×1 widget.
- **Clipboard** — your copied text, grouped by date and searchable, with an optional biometric lock.
- **Master Toy Hub** — cycle and customize the active Glyph toy on Phone (4a) Pro.

## Glyph toys

- **Glyph Snake** — the classic game on the rear LEDs, steered with the volume buttons, with a global high-score board.
- **Charging Hamster** — an animated hamster that runs when you plug in and shows battery level.
- **Day Matrix** — a 24-hour screen-time grid, one LED per hour, on the Glyph Matrix and AOD.
- **Where am I?** — shows your current neighbourhood name on the matrix when the phone is face down.

## Battery and privacy

Night Quiet Hours pauses background Glyph activity during your sleep schedule, which saves roughly 10–25% battery overnight. The optional permissions — accessibility for game controls and clipboard history, location for the place toy, and notifications for the music widget — are processed on the device. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
