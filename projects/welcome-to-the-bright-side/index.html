Welcome to the Bright Side

This project holds a special place in my heart—it's one of the largest personal endeavors I've ever undertaken. With the assistance of my two closest friends, I managed to bring it to life on a zero budget. The story revolves around the universe of Nothing and their product, the Nothing phone. The premise of this video is centered on someone discovering the world of phone 2 from the world of phone 1. Apart from acting and director of photography (DOP), I handled every aspect of its creation.
