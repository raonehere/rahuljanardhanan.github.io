Snake Widget for NothingOS

In January 2024 Rahul explored playful, useful widget ideas for Nothing OS, including a tiny home-screen version of the classic Snake game. He contributed the concept and the visual design — built with community developer Thomas Legendre — and it launched in December 2024 in the Nothing Community Widgets app and has crossed 100,000 downloads.
