Wild & Within

Svastha Healing Collective & EKA Forest

Three days to slow down in the forest at Vattavada. We arrive on Friday at 11 AM and leave on Sunday at 11 AM. In between there is sound and silence, gentle movement, a walk through the shola, and nights around the fire with the cold coming down off the ridges.

  • Awaken arriving, settling in, sound and breath
  • Explore walking the forest, farms and ridges
  • Restore yoga, rest and long meals together
  • Root quiet mornings and a slow goodbye

With Iyashi (sound), Root to Radiance and Flow State.

₹13,999 per person · tent stay and meals included
