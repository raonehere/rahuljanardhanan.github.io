Vattavada farms and a village walk

5 November 2026 · 3 min read

Vattavada looks different from Munnar because farming here is different. Instead of tea, the hills are terraced with vegetables, garlic and strawberries. A simple walk through the farms and the old lanes of Kovilur is often the best way to understand the village. Please check locally before you go for access and crop seasons.

Terraced fields and what grows here

The fields step down the hills in neat terraces. Carrot, cabbage, garlic and, in the right season, strawberries are among the crops people grow. What you see depends on the time of year and the individual farm, so a walk is about the pattern of the land as much as any single crop. Please check locally before you go if you hope to see a particular harvest.

Kovilur village lanes

Kovilur is the older village area near Vattavada, with quiet lanes and small houses set among trees. Walking here is slow and unplanned: there is no single route to follow, just lanes that open to views of the valley. Keep voices low, stay on public paths, and give way to residents and farm work.

Early morning is quietest

Many guests walk early, when mist is lifting and the farms are still. Light is good for photos and the air is cool. Carry a jacket and keep your phone torch off if you want to enjoy the surroundings. Farm paths can be narrow, muddy or uneven after rain, so wear suitable shoes.

Walking with respect

Farms are working places. Do not pick crops, enter fields without permission, or block paths used by residents. Ask before taking close photographs of people or homes. Please check locally before you go for any local guidance on walking routes and farm visits.
