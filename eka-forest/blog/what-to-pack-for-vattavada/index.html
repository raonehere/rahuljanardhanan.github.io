What to pack for Vattavada

5 November 2026 · 3 min read

Vattavada days are pleasant, but nights are cold all year. A small, sensible bag is enough. This list is general guidance for a tent stay at EKA Forest. Please check locally before you go, as needs vary by season and by person.

Layers for cold nights

Bring a warm jacket, a fleece or sweater, warm socks, a cap, and sleepwear that keeps heat in. Evenings around the fire can turn cold quickly once the sun drops behind the ridge. Gloves are optional, but many guests find them useful in December and January. Please check locally before you go if you are travelling outside those months, since night temperatures vary.

Footwear for farm and forest paths

Comfortable closed walking shoes with a good grip are enough for farm paths and village lanes. Waterproof socks or an extra dry pair can help on wet mornings. Sandals are fine for around the tents, but not for longer walks.

Light, medicines and small essentials

Carry a torch or headlamp for the path to the common washrooms at night, any regular medicines, a small water bottle, and chargers or a power bank for your phone. Mobile coverage can be patchy in the village, so download maps and keep travel tickets offline. Please check locally before you go for any connectivity needs.

For rain, mist and night sky time

Vattavada sits in a rain shadow and gets less rain than Munnar, but mist, drizzle and wind are part of hill life. A light windproof layer and a warm drink help during early mornings and stargazing. A blanket or shawl for sitting out after dark is useful. No guarantees apply to weather, mist or clear skies, so please check locally before you go.

What to leave at home

There is no need for heavy equipment or special gear. Keep the bag light: the stay is simple, meals are home-style in the outdoor dining area, and most time is spent walking, sitting and resting. Carry some cash as well, since card and UPI depend on the network.
