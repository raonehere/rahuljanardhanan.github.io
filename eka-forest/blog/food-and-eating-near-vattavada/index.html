Food at EKA Forest and eating near Vattavada

5 November 2026 · 3 min read

Many guests ask about food, so here is a plain account of how meals work at EKA Forest and in the village. Please check locally before you go, as menus and kitchen hours can change with season and supply.

Meals at EKA Forest

We serve simple home-style meals in the outdoor dining area. Breakfast, lunch and dinner are available on request, using vegetables from farms around Vattavada where possible. Let us know about dietary preferences or allergies when you book so we can plan around them, and carry any essentials you need from outside.

Eating in Vattavada

Vattavada is a small village, so choices outside the stay are limited. There are only a few small shops in the village, and options depend on the day. Most guests eat at the property and walk into the village for snacks or supplies rather than for full meals. Please check locally before you go if you need a specific diet or item.

Planning without guarantees

Supply from farms and shops varies with weather and harvest, and the same menu cannot be promised every day. If there is something you especially need, tell us before arrival. Carry snacks and water for the journey from Munnar and for any day trips. Details here are general guidance only. Please check locally before you go.
