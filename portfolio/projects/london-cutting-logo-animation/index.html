London Cutting / logo Animation

I created a logo animation for London Cutting, a chai brand inspired by the streets of London with a desi twist. The project was done in collaboration with Pixpil, and the goal was to bring out the brand’s bold, quirky identity through motion. It was a fun blend of culture, flavor, and design — and a great project to be part of.
