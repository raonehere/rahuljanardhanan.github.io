Kettle / Logo Animation

Kettle is a dating app built in Kerala that takes a fresh, thoughtful approach to how dating apps work. I got the chance to collaborate with the team at Pixpil Agency to create motion graphics for their logo and other visual elements in their brand system. It was a fun blend of playful design and local flavor.
