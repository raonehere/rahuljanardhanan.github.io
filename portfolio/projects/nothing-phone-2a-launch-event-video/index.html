Nothing Phone 2a / Launch Event Video

This is a video I made in collaboration with Nothing, a smartphone brand from London. My job was to create a video about the launch of their new smartphone, the ‘Phone 2a’. The challenge was to shoot the video using only the phone itself without any additional supporting hardware. I think I did a good job capturing the essence of the event and showcasing the capabilities of the device at the same time. And it was an incredibly rewarding and fun experience for me.
